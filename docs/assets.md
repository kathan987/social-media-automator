# Asset library

Every image, sticker, sound or clip you scrape or generate, logged once so later Shorts reuse it instead of paying
again. Files live in `../assets/`. The dashboard's Library page shows this table next to the files, and
`dashboard/research.py stock` appends its rows here automatically.

Rules:
- Check this list before scraping or generating anything.
- Official images keep their publisher URL; AI images keep their job ID; stickers keep their source URL. It all goes in
  the video description.
- Log what each asset cost.
